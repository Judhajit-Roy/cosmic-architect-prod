Physics sliders
These 5 values define the laws of your universe. Set them before you press Big Bang — they affect both the visual simulation and the AI's universe profile.
Explosion Force72
How violent the Big Bang is. High = fast, wide explosion. Low = gentle, dense core.
Gravity18
Attraction between particles and toward center. Low = particles fly apart freely.
Dark Energy65
Repulsive force pushing particles outward. High = runaway expanding universe.
Entropy40
Randomness of the explosion. High = chaotic, diffuse. Low = uniform, ordered.
Light Speed85
Maximum particle velocity. Scales time perception in the simulation.
Visual modes
Choose a mode before pressing Big Bang. Each changes how particles look and behave in the simulation.
STARS
Classic glowing spheres with soft blue-white halos. Particles pulse gently. Represents a standard stellar universe — galaxies, solar systems, star clusters.
Best with: medium gravity + medium force
NEBULA
Large diffuse clouds of ionized gas in purple/blue hues. Particles have bigger glow radius and trail faintly. Represents a young universe before stars condense.
Best with: low gravity + high entropy
BLACK HOLES
3 gravitational singularities spawn near the center. Nearby particles are sucked in and destroyed. Accretion disks glow amber-orange around each void.
Best with: high gravity + low dark energy
QUANTUM
Full-spectrum rainbow particles with visible motion trails. Erratic, probabilistic movement. Represents a universe at the subatomic scale where particles behave as waves.
Best with: max entropy + max light speed
